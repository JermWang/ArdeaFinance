BETA Private mode · powered by NEAR Intents

Cut the line between you and your trades.

A private buy never comes from your wallet. Your wallet pays a one-time deposit address, NEAR Intents pays a brand-new stealth wallet from its own liquidity, and that stealth wallet makes the trade.

A new wallet each buy

Every private buy runs from a stealth wallet with no history, so trackers see a stranger every time.

Confidential routing

Funds reach the stealth wallet through NEAR's Confidential Intents and are paid out by NEAR's bridge. Privatus doesn't run a mixer.

Recoverable anywhere

Stealth wallets come from one signature of your wallet. Sign again on any device to restore them. Nothing secret is stored.

No fee trail

The fee is paid from your main wallet when you fund. Stealth wallets never pay Privatus, so nothing on-chain marks them as ours.

How Private mode uses NEAR Intents

  1. Ask for a route. The terminal asks NEAR Intents' 1Click API to deliver SOL to a new stealth wallet, and gets back a one-time deposit address.
  2. Fund it once. You sign one transfer from your wallet to that address. Privatus's 0.5% fee rides in the same transaction.
  3. Solvers pay the stealth wallet. NEAR Intents fills the intent from its own liquidity. With Confidential Intents, the route stays off the public record.
  4. The stealth wallet buys. Once the SOL lands, it swaps through Jupiter. Exits run the same route in reverse, back to your wallet.
