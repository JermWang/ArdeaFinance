Route tracker

Track a route through Monero.

Every Ardea route (arming, a private buy, a disarm, an instant-wallet transfer) has its own id. Paste it or open its link to watch the route live. No wallet, no account.

The id lives after the # in the link, which browsers never send to a server. Your browser asks Houdini for the route through Ardea's gateway, which doesn't log it. Treat a tracking link like a receipt: anyone holding it can look up that route for 48 hours after it opens, so share it only on purpose. Houdini keeps route data for 72 hours.
