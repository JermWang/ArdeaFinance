    Arm a clean wallet. Fire private buys instantly.

    Fund fresh wallets through Monero ahead of time. When you spot a coin, an armed wallet buys it in one click: no route to wait for, and because the funding happened earlier, the buy's timing and amount don't line up with your deposit. With Private mode on, every buy and every quick-buy fires the next armed wallet.

    1. ArmPick a total and how many wallets. You approve once; each wallet gets its own Monero route, sent at random intervals.
    2. Wait onceSOL becomes XMR, crosses Monero, and an unrelated exchange pays each wallet. 10–40 minutes, in the background.
    3. FireAny buy fires the oldest armed wallet with its whole balance, signed in this tab. Disarm any time: it returns through Monero.
